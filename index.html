About

I study how to make AI interpretable and verifiable when it is applied to quantum and physical systems — from Markov state models of photosynthetic proteins to quantum algorithms for excited states.

Research

ICFO · Lewenstein & Liguori groups · May 2025 – present

Deep Markov state models of photoprotection in plants

How PsbS/LHCII switches from light harvesting to photoprotection. A five-layer VAMPnet learns nonlinear slow coordinates from coarse-grained MD across three pH conditions; an out-of-core TICA keeps full residue-level resolution.

  • 175 trajectories
  • 4,500 µs
  • 750k × 26.8k features (80 GB)
PyTorch · deeptime · MDTraj · Chapman–Kolmogorov validation

Florida Poly · PI Dr. Bradford Barker · Feb 2024 – present

q-sc-SF-EOM: a spin-flip quantum algorithm for ground and excited states

Resolves ground and excited singlets in a single diagonalization from a high-spin reference. A completeness framework predicts spin contamination a priori from the fraction of the MS = 0 space each operator manifold spans.

  • H₂: 52 → 16 CNOTs
  • H₄ manifold 9× smaller
  • 5 molecules
Qiskit · PySCF · poster at IEEE QCE26

Independent research · Jul 2026

Agentic MSM benchmark engine

A multi-agent LLM system (orchestrator / optimizer / validator) that designs analysis pipelines for noisy simulations. A deterministic physics gate — not the model — accepts results; ground-truth constants are formally verified in Lean 4.

  • rates within 1.87% of exact
  • 4/4 runs converged
  • 20 physics rejections
Lean 4 / Mathlib · LLM APIs · Pydantic
